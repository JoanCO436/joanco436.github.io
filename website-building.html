Website building

Clean, professional websites for individuals and small businesses — portfolios, landing pages, and simple business sites built to look sharp, read well, and be easy to update.

How I help

You don't need something complicated — you need a site that looks professional, says the right things, and works on a phone. That's what I build.

From a one-page portfolio to a small business site, I handle the layout, wording, and setup, and I'll show you how to make simple updates yourself afterwards so you're never stuck.

What's included

  • Personal portfolios and profile sites
  • Landing pages for a service or launch
  • Simple small-business websites
  • Clear, professional copy for each page
  • Mobile-friendly layouts that look good everywhere
  • A quick handover so you can update it yourself

Good fit if…

You want a clean, professional website without the jargon or the cost of a big agency — and a bit of guidance to keep it updated.
